### About Me

I am a Data Science graduate with a deep fascination for how systems learn, adapt, and interact. My journey into technology started with a simple curiosity about data and has evolved into a focused pursuit of artificial intelligence and machine learning. 

I believe the best technology doesn't just compute—it understands context and solves real human problems. Right now, my focus is heavily tilted towards multimodal AI and natural language processing. Whether I'm trying to forecast student engagement or building sentiment analyzers, my goal is always to create models that are as intuitive as they are technically sound.

When I'm not writing code or reading up on the latest ML architectures, I spend my time exploring how we can bridge the gap between complex data and everyday utility. I'm always open to discussing new ideas, collaborating on challenging problems, or just chatting about the future of tech.
